## Scope

The whole app (one surface): first-run goal picking, the set, logging, the day strip, the film. Mode: Experience. Unattended run: Karim's standing rule is no questions, so the decision page was not served; the calls below are mine.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A brickfilm set. Every win is a toy brick snapped onto its goal's tower; towers only grow; the whole build replays as a 12 fps stop-motion film that plays each brick as a note. Refuses the category default (streak flames, checklists, a contribution heatmap, bar charts).

OWN-WORLD: A lit miniature set. Warm studio-gray paper sweep, a dark bluish-gray baseplate plinth with studs, glossy ABS bricks (clearcoat plastic, soft PCSS shadows, ambient occlusion in the seams, faint tilt-shift). Colour lives only in the bricks: a curated toy palette, one colour per goal, never tinted for state. UI is two inks on the set: warm ink #232420 and one signal orange #F04A00 reserved for the film transport and the selected-day mark. Type: Google Sans Flex, rounded axis, heavy numerals. Icons: Lucide, one stroke.

STORY: He sees his towers, sees what today added, drops a brick for a win in one gesture, and at the end of a week presses play and watches everything build itself.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL set, towers in a row on the plinth, slightly above centre. Top left: the step numeral (day N of building, about 88px) with the date under it and today's parts listed beneath as small drawn bricks with counts. Top right: film and sound buttons. Bottom centre: the goal pads (the primary action), with the day strip just above them.

FORM: Candidate 6 of my ordered list (a pocket step-sequencer) was assigned; the brief pins the material to its own named directions (stop motion plus toy bricks), so the sequencer binds topology, controls and ritual: goals are tracks (towers), days are steps (time runs upward, and along the day strip), pads record a hit whose hold length is the brick size (1x2, 2x2, 2x4), transport plays the build back with a playhead, every brick is a note. Seed key acbb8373.

RAISES:
- From the brick instruction book (competitive): the step numeral leads the type, the day's new parts sit in a 1:1 call-out with counts, UI spacing runs on an 8px stud module.
- From the cutting-bench rail (declined): the day strip scrubs with inertia and settles on whole days, never between; the selected day is marked, not tinted.
- From the illuminated initial (declined): deep zoom toward the cursor, from the whole set down to one brick, whose date and note show on hover.
- From the paper automata (declined): one thing moves at a time; the set holds still while a brick is placed.
- From the rebel menu (declined): the film starts and ends on hard cuts, no fades.
- From the arcade cabinet (declined): palette law; goal colours are never tinted for state, and only gold is reserved, for every 100th brick of a goal.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
