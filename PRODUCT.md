Karim's standing rule is no questions back, so this record comes from his brief of 2026-09-23 alone. Lines marked (inferred) are calls made for him, not answers he gave.

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite, React, TypeScript, three.js through React Three Fiber and drei, postprocessing. He named three.js, R3F and motion.dev in the brief. Hosted on Vercel (https://brickwork-iota.vercel.app) with one serverless function and a private Vercel Blob for sync, at his request on 2026-09-23. Source: github.com/karimbabasf/brickwork (private).

## Users

Karim, one person, logging his own day. He opens it at the end of a block or the end of the day to put down what he got done, and on slow days to look back at how much has piled up. (inferred: the moment of use)

## Product Purpose

A place to put what you have done and watch it compound. Not a planner: nothing in it looks forward, sets targets or nags. Success is that logging a win takes one gesture and looking at the whole thing makes him smile.

## Positioning

Every win becomes a physical object that stays. The view is the sum of everything logged, built up over time, per goal, so compounding is something you see and can replay, not a number.

## Operating Context

Desktop browser on his Mac, sometimes a phone. Short sessions, several times a day. (inferred)

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Onboarding sets up a handful of goals (topics) with almost no typing.
- Logging is one gesture per win, with the size of the win as the only input. A short note is optional, never required.
- Two views: today, and everything over time across goals.
- One private log syncs across his devices (asked for on 2026-09-23: "host it on vercel... offload anything local"). No accounts: a secret sync link opened once per device. Without the link the app works saved in that browser only.
- Must stay smooth with years of entries (thousands of bricks). (inferred)

## Brand Commitments

- Feel: warm and pleasant, makes you smile, a bit like a stop-motion film, but not flickery.
- He named four directions he likes: stop motion, 3D toy building blocks with a rendered finish, mouse-reactive water, trees and leaves with zoom. The build takes the first two together.
- Type from his shortlist only; no display serifs.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. The synthetic demo history was removed on 2026-09-23 at his request; never add fake entries to the live app.

## Product Principles

1. Doing, not planning: the app only ever records the past.
2. One gesture to log, zero forms.
3. Nothing is ever lost or shrinks: the build only grows.
4. The payoff is looking at it.
